Refund & Cancellation Policy 退款及取消政策

Cancellations made on or before June 15 (14 days before camp) are eligible for a full refund. 6月15日或之前（開營前14天）取消，可获全額退款。
Cancellations made after June 15 are non-refundable. However, your spot may be transferred to another student — please contact us to arrange. 6月15日之後取消，恕不退款。但名額可自行轉讓，聯繫我們辦理。
A minimum of 8 students is required to run the camp. If this is not met, all registered students will receive a full refund. 須滿 8人 方可成班。如未达人数下限，所有已報名學員将获全額退款。
If the camp is cancelled by the organiser due to circumstances beyond our control, a full refund will be issued. 如因不可抗力由主辦方取消營地，将向所有學員發放全額退款。
